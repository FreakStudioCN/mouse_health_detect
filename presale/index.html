Straight from the test bench.

A frame from our 384-hour continuous run: four pigs, told apart and tracked, behaviour read — all computed on the box itself.

Brain
CanMV K230D — RISC-V with a 1-TOPS NPU. The 1.2 MB model runs at 13 fps on the box itself.
Eyes
1080p camera (GC2093) with infrared night vision.
Ears
On-board microphone sampled at 44.1 kHz, listening for breathing sounds.
Power & link
USB-C, about 3 W — any phone charger. Wi-Fi that reconnects itself.
Testing
384 hours of continuous running; detection checked against 25,000+ hand-labelled frames.
Privacy
Video is analysed on the device. The full device privacy policy comes before anything ships.
